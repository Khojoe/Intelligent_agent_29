
# Campus Information Bot — Knowledge Base

**Note:** All information below is fictional and created
for this laboratory exercise. It does not represent
actual University of Ghana office information.

## Campus Office Information

1. Department Office:
   The Department Office is located in Block A,
   Room 105, on the North Campus.

2. Opening Hours:
   The Department Office operates from Monday to Friday,
   between 8:00 AM and 5:00 PM.

3. Academic Advising:
   Students seeking academic advice can visit
   Block A, Room 110, from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM,
   Monday to Thursday.

4. ICT Support:
   The ICT Support Office is located in Block B,
   Room 202. It assists students with internet,
   computer and account-related problems.

5. Library:
   The Main Library is located opposite Block C.
   It operates from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on weekdays.

6. Admissions Office:
   The Admissions Office is located in Block A,
   Room 101. It handles admission enquiries
   and application-related questions.

7. Examination Office:
   The Examination Office is located in Block C,
   Room 105. It handles examination timetables,
   examination records and related enquiries.

8. Student Support:
   Students requiring general welfare assistance
   can visit the Student Support Office in Block D,
   Room 103, between 9:00 AM and 4:00 PM.
